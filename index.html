Usamat & Hauwa Naali

The story of a family from Keana — its parents, its six children, and the ties of respect and togetherness that hold them together.

Where the family comes from

A home built on Keana's ground

The Naali family belongs to Keana Local Government Area in Nasarawa State, a place the family describes as blessed with the salt-production heritage of their ancestors. Alhaji Usamat Aliyu Naali and Hajiya Hauwa Jibrin Sahara are at the center of a home shaped by faith, education, respect, and togetherness.

Father of the house

Alhaji Usamat Aliyu Naali

Of the Royal Family of Osana Agbo

The family traces his lineage to the Royal Family of Osana Agbo, and he is remembered as connected to the late King of Keana. A retired civil servant, he is the father of six children and the anchor of the family’s traditions and values.

Mother of the house

Hajiya Hauwa Jibrin Sahara

Educated in Jos, Nigeria

A civil servant who pursued her studies in Jos, Hauwa Jibrin Sahara is the mother of six children, three sons and three daughters, and wife to Usamat Aliyu Naali.

What holds the family together

Values and tradition

Togetherness

The family holds togetherness as central to how they live, across eight members and however far each child's path may take them.

Respect for one another

Respect between parents and children, and among the six siblings, is a value the family names as its own.

The Eid gathering

On the fourth day of Eid Mubarak, the family comes together for its general family meeting, a tradition kept each year.

A shared home

Keana remains the family's home ground, the place its parents and children return to and carry with them.
